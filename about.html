Karin’s Farm turns the classic secret-role party game into a bright, silly farmyard adventure. Your friends might be a chicken, a cow, or something a little wilder in disguise.

Every round is different. Players listen, bluff, ask questions, and try to spot what doesn’t quite add up. It’s a little mystery that gives everyone a reason to join in.
